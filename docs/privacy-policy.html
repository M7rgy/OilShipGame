Hormuz Escape

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 July 2026

Hormuz Escape is a single-player arcade game developed by M7rgy. This policy explains what information the app handles and why. In short: the game itself collects nothing about you, and the only data leaving your device is what Google AdMob needs to show ads.

Information the game collects

The developer does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The game has no accounts, no logins, and no servers of its own.

Your settings and high scores are saved only on your device (in local storage) so the game remembers them between sessions. This data never leaves your device and is removed if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Advertising

Hormuz Escape shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google's advertising SDK may collect and process information such as your device's advertising identifier (Advertising ID), general device and app information, and coarse, non-precise usage data. This is used to display ads, limit repetition, and prevent fraud.

This processing is carried out by Google, not by the developer. Google's handling of that data is governed by their own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

You can reset or limit ad personalization at any time from your device's Settings → Google → Ads.

Children

Hormuz Escape is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. Ads are configured for a general audience.

Permissions

The app requests internet access, which is required only to load ads. It does not access your contacts, photos, location, microphone, or camera.

Data sharing & retention

The developer does not sell or share any personal data, because none is collected. Advertising data handled by Google is retained and managed according to Google's policies linked above.

Changes to this policy
